Amlin vs Wispr Flow

Both apps let you hold a key, talk, and get clean text typed wherever your cursor is. Wispr Flow does the work on its servers and runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android. Amlin does it on your Mac, charges nothing for dictation, and writes up your meetings in the same app.

Facts checked November 2026 against Wispr Flow's public pricing, help center, and privacy documentation. Spot something outdated? Tell us.

The short answer

Wispr Flow is a voice dictation app for Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android. It removes filler words, punctuates, adjusts tone, and understands more than 100 languages. Your voice is transcribed on Wispr's servers, so it needs an internet connection. In August 2026 it added Notetaker, which writes meeting notes with no bot in the call.

Amlin is a native Mac app with three tools in one: dictation that runs entirely on your Mac, a meeting notepad that never stores audio, and a to-do list filled from your meetings.

On price: Amlin's dictation is free with no word limit, and Pro is $10 a month or $108 a year. Wispr Flow's free plan limits how much you can dictate each week; Flow Pro is $15 a month, or $144 a year.

Which one fits you

Choose Amlin if…

  • You dictate client names, contract terms, or unreleased work and want the audio to stay on your Mac.
  • You'd like to dictate all day without watching a weekly word count.
  • You also sit in meetings and want notes, dictation, and a to-do list sharing one dictionary for $10 a month.
  • You dictate on planes, trains, or patchy Wi-Fi. Amlin's dictation needs no connection.

Choose Wispr Flow if…

  • You need Windows, iPhone, or Android, or an Intel Mac; Amlin runs on Apple silicon Macs only.
  • You dictate in several languages; Wispr Flow understands more than 100.
  • You want to select a paragraph and say "make this shorter". Command Mode does that, and Amlin has no equivalent.

Questions, answered

Is Amlin a good Wispr Flow alternative?

If you work on an Apple silicon Mac and want your dictation to stay on the machine, yes. Amlin's dictation runs on-device, is included in the free plan, and is never metered, and the same app writes up your meetings and keeps a to-do list across them. Wispr Flow is the better fit if you need Windows, iPhone, or Android, dictate in many languages, or want to edit selected text by voice with Command Mode.

Does Wispr Flow process dictation on your device?

No. Wispr Flow sends your audio to its servers for transcription and needs an internet connection; Wispr says it offers no offline mode. A setting called Privacy Mode asks Wispr and its providers to discard dictation data after processing, and it is off by default on individual accounts, so it is worth switching on if you use Flow. Amlin's dictation runs entirely on your Mac: the audio never leaves the device, and the cleanup happens there too. The details are in our privacy policy.

Is Wispr Flow free?

There is a free plan, called Basic, with a limited amount of dictation each week. Wispr's help center describes it as limited dictation, and reviewers put the desktop allowance at 2,000 words a week, which is about 13 minutes of speaking. Flow Pro removes the limit for $15 a month, or $144 a year. Amlin's dictation has no word limit on any plan, including the free one; the free plan's 4 meeting-hours apply to meeting transcription only.

Does Wispr Flow take meeting notes?

Yes. Wispr added Notetaker in August 2026, first on Mac and then on Windows. Like Amlin, it records from the sound your computer plays, so no bot joins the call and guests install nothing. The two differ in what happens to the audio. Wispr describes meeting audio as uploaded and retained temporarily, with no way to replay it. Amlin transcribes live and discards the audio on the spot, so only your notes and the transcript text remain. Amlin also gathers action items from every meeting into one list with owners and due dates.

Does dictation cost extra in Amlin?

No. System-wide dictation is included in the free plan and never metered. Amlin Pro, at $10 a month or $108 a year, raises the meeting allowance from 4 to 40 hours a month and makes Ask unlimited; dictation is the same on both plans.
