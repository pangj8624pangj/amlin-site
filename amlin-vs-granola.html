Amlin vs Granola

Both are meeting notepads: you jot rough notes during the call, no bot joins, and AI writes them up afterward. They differ on price, platforms, privacy details, and how much of your workday they cover.

Facts checked August 2026 against each company's public pricing and documentation. Spot something outdated? Tell us.

The short answer

Granola is built for teams and runs everywhere: Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android, with shared notes and integrations, at $14 per user per month on the Business plan. Amlin is built for one person on a Mac at $10 a month, and folds dictation and a self-filling to-do list into the same app. Granola's free plan limits how far back you can revisit notes; Amlin's free plan limits monthly meeting-hours and keeps your history forever.

Which one fits you

Choose Amlin if…

  • You work on a Mac and want notes, dictation, and to-dos handled by one $10 app instead of two or three subscriptions.
  • "We never store your audio" and on-device dictation matter to you or to your clients.
  • You want every commitment from every call collected into a single list with owners and due dates.
  • You want your full note history on the free plan while you evaluate.

Choose Granola if…

  • You need Windows, iPhone, or Android alongside (or instead of) a Mac.
  • Your team shares meeting notes, and you want them flowing into Notion, HubSpot, or Slack.
  • You want unlimited meetings on a free plan and don't mind losing easy access to older notes.

Questions, answered

Is Amlin a good Granola alternative?

If you work solo on a Mac, yes. Amlin covers the same core job (bot-free meeting notes enhanced by AI) at $10 a month instead of $14, and adds system-wide dictation and a cross-meeting to-do list. If you need Windows, iPhone, or Android apps, or team features like shared notes and Notion or HubSpot integrations, Granola is the better fit, and we'd rather tell you that here than have you find out after a download.

Does Granola or Amlin send a bot into meetings?

Neither. Both apps capture audio from your computer at the operating-system level, so no bot participant joins the call and other attendees have nothing to install. This is the trait the two apps share most deeply, and it's why people comparing them are usually deciding between these two rather than bot-based tools.

Can I try both for free?

Yes, and you probably should. Granola's free Basic plan allows unlimited meetings but restricts how far back you can revisit notes until you upgrade. Amlin's free plan includes 4 meeting-hours per month (about 5 or 6 meetings), unmetered dictation, and keeps your full note history.

Does Amlin work on Windows or iPhone?

No. Amlin is a macOS app (macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon) and that's the honest limit of this comparison: if your work happens outside a Mac, Granola is the one to pick today.
