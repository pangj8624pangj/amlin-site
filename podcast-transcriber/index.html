Podcast Transcriber

Turn podcast episodes, YouTube videos, and your own recordings into searchable transcripts and summaries. It runs on your Mac, with no account or subscription.

Download for Mac

508 MB · Apple Silicon (M1 or later) · Signed and notarized by Apple

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later). Intel Macs are not supported.
  • 16 GB of memory recommended.
  • About 5 GB of free disk space, plus room for any audio you keep.
  • An internet connection to find episodes and download the AI models on first use.

Install

  1. Download the file. Google Drive may say it can’t scan a file this large, so choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the DMG and drag Podcast Transcriber to Applications.
  3. Open the app from Applications. If macOS asks to confirm, click Open.

Transcribe your first episode

  1. Click New Transcription or press ⌘N.
  2. Paste one or more Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, or RSS links, one per line. You can also add an audio or video file.
  3. Click Transcribe. If a link could match more than one episode, choose the right one.
  4. When it finishes, open the episode from your Library.

The first run takes longer while the models download. After that, an hour of audio takes roughly 3 to 4 minutes on an M4 Mac. Older Macs take longer.

What it can do

Transcripts with timestamps

Read along, search, and play the audio from the app.

Speaker labels

See who is talking, then replace “Speaker 1” with a real name.

AI summaries

Get key takeaways and verified quotes, generated on your Mac.

One-click copy

Copy the transcript, a clean version, the summary, or everything.

Highlights and notes

Save quotes with notes, then export to Notion, Google Docs, Slack, or Markdown.

Follow shows

New episodes transcribe automatically while the app is open.

Organize your library

Star episodes, add tags, and filter by podcast.

Re-transcribe

Rebuild an episode with your current settings. The earlier version is saved.

Transcript limit

Keep only your latest transcripts. Starred and highlighted episodes stay.

Connect AI apps

Let Claude Desktop or Codex search your transcripts.

Privacy and cost

Transcription, speaker labels, and the default summaries are free and run on your Mac. Your files are saved in Documents > Podcast Transcripts.

Claude summaries are optional. If you turn them on, you add your own Anthropic API key, your transcript is sent to Anthropic, and usage is billed to your account. A one-hour episode costs about $0.20 to $0.25.

Good to know

  • Transcripts and summaries are machine-generated, so check anything important against the audio.
  • Following only works while the app is open.
  • Only transcribe content you have the right to use.
