No recording of a customer's voice

Prospects share budget, timelines, and what they think of your competitors. Amlin never stores audio. Sound is transcribed live and discarded, so no recording of the customer's voice sits on a server or in a folder. What remains is text: your notes and the transcript, synced to your own account. The privacy policy explains it in plain language when a security reviewer asks.

Every next step, with a date

"I'll send the security questionnaire by Wednesday." "Can you loop in your finance lead?" Amlin pulls each commitment out of the call with an owner and a due date and keeps them in one list across all your accounts, split into what you owe and what you are waiting on from others.

Ask about a call from last quarter

Ask across your history: "which accounts mentioned a renewal date in March?" or "what did the buyer say about pricing last time?" Each answer links to the exact line it came from, so you can check it before you quote it back to the customer.

The follow-up email, drafted aloud

The summary and action items appear seconds after the call ends. Hold the dictation key and talk through your recap to the customer, or your handoff note to a teammate. Dictation runs on your Mac, formats the greeting and paragraphs for an email, and is included free with no monthly cap.

Where Amlin does not fit

Amlin is a personal notes tool for a Mac with Apple silicon. It has no CRM integration, so you copy what you need into your own system. It does not coach you live, score calls, or share a call library with a team. If your company requires recorded calls for training or review, its approved system covers that, and Amlin keeps no audio to offer. It also has no iPhone or Windows app.

Questions sales reps ask us

Will the customer see anything on the call?

No. Amlin runs on your Mac and captures the sound your Mac plays plus your microphone, so no bot joins and the customer sees no extra attendee. Recording-consent rules vary by place, and our terms make telling participants and following the rules that apply to your calls your responsibility. Many reps open with a sentence like "I take notes with a tool on my own computer."

Where do the notes and transcripts live?

Notes and transcript text sync to your own account so your other Macs can reach them. Meeting audio is transcribed live and discarded, so no recording of the customer's voice exists afterward. Deleting a note deletes it from our servers too. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Does it connect to my CRM?

No. Amlin has no integration with Salesforce, HubSpot, or any other CRM. Notes live in Amlin, and you copy the summary or the fields you want into your CRM. If your team requires call notes in a particular system, keep entering them there.

Does it work with Zoom, Meet, and Teams calls?

It works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and anything else that makes sound on the Mac. There is no per-platform integration to break, and the customer never has to approve a bot to join.

Can I see everything I promised one account?

Every call is its own note, tied to the calendar event when you connect Google Calendar (read-only and optional). Ask can be scoped to a single call, and the action-item list shows each commitment with an owner and a due date, so you can scan what you owe before the next call.

What does it cost?

The Free plan includes 4 meeting-hours per month with full note history. Pro is $10 per month or $108 per year. Dictation is included free and is never metered.
