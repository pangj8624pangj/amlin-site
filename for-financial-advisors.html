Amlin for financial advisors

Your client talks about money.
No bot is listening in.

People tell their advisor about a divorce, an inheritance, or a health scare. A bot called "Notetaker AI" appearing on that call changes how freely they talk. Amlin captures the conversation from your own Mac, so the client sees one attendee: you, looking at them instead of at your keyboard.

Less sensitive material left behind

Client conversations cover balances, family details, and goals nobody wants floating around. Amlin never stores audio. Sound is transcribed live and discarded, so no recording of your client's voice sits on a server or in a folder. What remains is text: your notes and the transcript, synced to your own account. The privacy policy explains it in plain language when a client asks.

Every promise, with a date

"I'll send the rollover paperwork Thursday." "Let me check the beneficiary on that account." Amlin pulls each commitment out of the meeting with an owner and a due date and keeps them in one list across all your clients, split into what you owe and what you are waiting on from them.

Ask about last year's meeting

Ask across your history: "what did the Hendersons say about college costs?" or "when did she first mention retiring early?" Each answer links to the exact line it came from, so you can check it before you repeat it to the client.

The follow-up email, drafted aloud

The summary, decisions, and action items appear seconds after the call ends. Hold the dictation key and talk through your follow-up email. Dictation runs on your Mac, formats the greeting and paragraphs for an email, and is included free with no monthly cap.

Where Amlin does not fit

Amlin is a personal notes tool for a Mac with Apple silicon. It makes no compliance claims. If your firm or regulator requires recorded calls or archived communications, your firm's approved system covers that, and Amlin keeps no audio to offer. It also has no iPhone or Windows app.

Questions financial advisors ask us

Do my clients need to install or approve anything?

No. Amlin runs on your Mac and captures the sound your Mac plays plus your microphone, so nothing joins the call and the client sees no extra attendee. Recording-consent rules vary by place, and our terms make telling participants and following the rules that apply to your calls your responsibility. Many advisors open with one sentence such as "I take notes with a tool on my own computer."

Where do the notes and transcripts live?

Notes and transcript text sync to your own account so your other Macs can reach them. Meeting audio is transcribed live and discarded, so no recording of the client's voice exists afterward. Deleting a note deletes it from our servers too. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Can Amlin replace my firm's required record-keeping?

Check with your firm first. Amlin keeps text notes and a transcript, and it never keeps audio. If your firm or your regulator requires specific records, such as recorded calls or archived communications, follow your firm's policy and use the system it approves. Amlin makes no compliance claims and is a personal notes tool.

Does it work with whatever platform the client picks?

Yes. Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and anything else that makes sound on the Mac. There is no per-platform integration to break.

Can I keep one client's notes apart from another's?

Every meeting is its own note, tied to the calendar event when you connect Google Calendar (read-only and optional). Ask can be scoped to a single meeting when you want answers from one client's conversation only.

What does it cost?

The Free plan includes 4 meeting-hours per month with full note history. Pro is $10 per month or $108 per year. Dictation is included free and is never metered.
