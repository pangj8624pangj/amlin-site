Amlin vs Fireflies.ai

Fireflies is built around a bot. The Fireflies Notetaker joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams call as a participant, records it, and files the recording in a cloud workspace your team can search. Amlin is a notepad on your own Mac: no bot in the call, no audio stored, and dictation and to-dos in the same app.

Facts checked November 2026 against Fireflies' public pricing and help center. Spot something outdated? Tell us.

The short answer

Fireflies is a cloud meeting recorder you reach through the web, iPhone and Android apps, a Chrome extension, and a Mac/Windows desktop app. The default path is the bot: it joins the call, records audio (and video on paid plans), and the recording lives in your account. Plans meter recording storage in minutes and AI features in credits.

Amlin is a native Mac app that captures the call from your own machine, never stores audio, and includes system-wide dictation and a cross-meeting to-do list.

On price: Amlin is $10 a month or $108 a year. Fireflies Pro is $18 a month, or $10 a month billed annually ($120 a year). Teams that want shared channels, CRM syncing, and call analytics pay $19 per seat billed annually for Fireflies Business.

Which one fits you

Choose Amlin if…

  • You don't want a client to find "Fireflies Notetaker" waiting in the participant list.
  • A recording of the call should never exist anywhere. Amlin keeps text only, and dictation runs on-device.
  • You want dictation in every Mac app and a cross-meeting to-do list from the same $10 app.
  • You pay month to month and would rather pay $10 than $18.

Choose Fireflies if…

  • You need Windows, iPhone, Android, or the browser; Amlin is Mac-only.
  • You want playback of the original recording, or video of the call, which requires storing it.
  • Your team searches one shared library of calls, syncs notes to a CRM, and reads call analytics.
  • You transcribe a very large volume of meetings and want the bot to auto-join every calendar invite.

Questions, answered

Is Amlin a good Fireflies.ai alternative?

If you work on a Mac and don't want a bot in your meetings, yes. Amlin captures the sound your Mac plays from your own machine, so nothing joins the call, audio is never stored, and dictation and a cross-meeting to-do list come in the same app. Fireflies is the better fit if you need Windows, iPhone, Android, or browser access, want playback of recordings, or run a team that searches one shared library of calls.

Does Amlin join meetings the way the Fireflies Notetaker does?

No. By default, the Fireflies Notetaker joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams call as a visible participant that other attendees see and sometimes have to admit; many people invite it by adding fred@fireflies.ai to the calendar event. Amlin never uses a bot: it captures the audio your Mac plays, so the participant list always shows only people, and other attendees have nothing to admit or install.

Can Fireflies record without a bot?

Yes, with limits. The Fireflies desktop app for Mac and Windows has a Take Notes mode that captures your computer's audio with no bot in the call, and the Chrome extension records Google Meet without a bot. In Take Notes mode, speakers are labeled generically (Speaker 1, Speaker 2) and there is no video. The bot remains the default path and the one Fireflies' auto-join feature uses, so you have to pick the bot-free mode each time you want it.

Does Fireflies or Amlin store meeting recordings?

Fireflies keeps recordings of bot-captured meetings in your account so you can play them back and share clips; video recording is included on paid plans, and storage is metered in minutes per plan. That playback is a real feature, and it requires storing the recording. Amlin makes the opposite trade: sound is transcribed live and discarded on the spot, so there is no recording to leak, subpoena, or worry about. Only your notes and the transcript text remain. The full picture is in our privacy policy.

How do the free plans compare?

Fireflies' free plan offers unlimited transcription as long as auto-join is on, which means the bot joins every meeting on your calendar. It includes 400 minutes of recording storage that does not reset monthly, 20 AI credits a month for summaries and AskFred, and no video or transcript downloads. Amlin's free plan includes 4 meeting-hours a month, a meeting in progress always finishes, your full note history is kept, and dictation is included and never metered.
