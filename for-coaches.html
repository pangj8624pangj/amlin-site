No recording of a client's voice

Clients talk about money, family, and what they fear. Amlin never stores audio. Sound is transcribed live and discarded, so no recording of your client sits on a server or in a folder. What remains is text: your notes and the transcript, synced to your own account. The privacy policy explains it in plain language when a client asks.

Every commitment, with a date

"I'll have the conversation with my manager by the 15th." "Send me the reading list." Amlin pulls each commitment out of the session with an owner and a due date and keeps them in one list across all your clients, split into what you owe and what clients owe you. Open the list before the next session and start with the follow-through.

Ask what a client said in March

Ask across your history: "when did Priya first mention leaving the firm?" or "which clients brought up burnout this quarter?" Each answer links to the exact line it came from, so you can check the wording before you quote it back to someone.

The recap email, spoken in a minute

The summary and action items appear seconds after the session ends. Hold the dictation key and talk through your recap and the homework you set. Dictation runs on your Mac, formats the greeting and paragraphs for an email, and is included free with no monthly cap.

Where Amlin does not fit

Amlin is a personal notes tool for a Mac with Apple silicon. It makes no clinical, therapeutic, or medical claims and has no features built for regulated record-keeping, so if your profession requires specific records, use the system it requires. There is no client portal or shared workspace, and no iPhone or Windows app.

Questions coaches ask us

Will my client see anything on the call?

No. Amlin runs on your Mac and captures the sound your Mac plays plus your microphone, so no bot joins and your client sees no extra attendee. Recording-consent rules vary by place, and our terms make telling participants and following the rules that apply to your sessions your responsibility. One way to cover it is to say once: "I take written notes with a tool on my own computer, and no audio is kept."

Where do the notes and transcripts live?

Notes and transcript text sync to your own account so your other Macs can reach them. Session audio is transcribed live and discarded, so no recording of your client's voice exists afterward. Deleting a note deletes it from our servers too. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Can I share my notes with a client?

Notes are written for you. When you want to send a client their commitments or a recap, copy the summary or the action items into an email, or dictate the message after the session. Amlin has no client portal or shared workspace.

Is this suitable for therapy or medical sessions?

Amlin makes no claims about clinical or health use. It is a personal notes tool, and it does not provide record-keeping features built for therapy, medical, or other regulated practices. If your work falls under professional record rules, use the system your profession requires.

Can I keep one client's notes apart from another's?

Every session is its own note, tied to the calendar event when you connect Google Calendar (read-only and optional). Ask can be scoped to a single session when you want answers from one conversation only, or run across your history to find which clients mentioned a topic.

What does it cost?

The Free plan includes 4 meeting-hours per month with full note history. Pro is $10 per month or $108 per year. Dictation is included free and is never metered.
