Amlin vs tl;dv

tl;dv is a meeting recorder. Its bot joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams call, records video, and files it in a cloud library where you can replay moments and share clips. Amlin is a notepad on your own Mac: no bot in the call, no audio stored, and dictation and to-dos in the same app.

Facts checked November 2026 against tl;dv's public pricing and help center. Spot something outdated? Tell us.

The short answer

tl;dv records your calls on video through a bot, transcribes them in more than 30 languages, and writes AI notes you can search and push to other tools. It runs in the browser, as a Chrome extension, and as a Mac/Windows desktop app that can record audio without the bot.

Amlin is a native Mac app that captures the call from your own machine, never stores audio, and includes system-wide dictation and a cross-meeting to-do list.

On price: Amlin is $10 a month or $108 a year. tl;dv Pro is $29 per seat a month, or $18 per seat a month billed annually ($216 a year). Business, with team features, is $29 per seat a month billed annually.

Which one fits you

  • No video or audio of the call should exist anywhere. Amlin keeps text only, and dictation runs on-device.
  • You want a free allowance that renews every month; tl;dv's free AI notes stop after 10 meetings for good.

Questions, answered

Is Amlin a good tl;dv alternative?

If you work on a Mac and don't want a recorder in your meetings, yes. Amlin captures the sound your Mac plays from your own machine, so nothing joins the call, audio is never stored, and dictation and a cross-meeting to-do list come in the same app. tl;dv is the better fit if you need Windows or a browser, want video playback and clips of your calls, or transcribe calls in many languages.

Does tl;dv join meetings as a bot?

By default, yes. tl;dv's recorder joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls as a participant that other attendees can see, and in some calls the host has to let it in. Amlin never uses a bot: it listens to the sound your Mac plays, so the participant list shows only people, and nobody else has anything to admit or install.

Can tl;dv record without a bot?

Yes. The tl;dv desktop app for Mac and Windows records your computer's audio and microphone with no bot in the call, and it works with any meeting tool that plays sound. It captures audio only, with no video, screen share, or chat, so the clip and replay features built around video don't apply. On a Mac you grant it Screen Recording and Microphone permissions in System Settings first.

Does tl;dv or Amlin store meeting recordings?

tl;dv keeps video recordings of bot-captured meetings in your account so you can replay them and cut clips. That replay is the heart of the product, and it requires storing the recording. On the free plan, recordings move to archived storage after 3 days; the notes stay readable, and you unarchive a recording to watch it. Amlin makes the opposite trade: sound is transcribed live and discarded on the spot, so no recording exists to leak or hand over. Only your notes and the transcript text remain. The details are in our privacy policy.

How do the free plans compare?

tl;dv's free plan includes unlimited recordings and transcripts, but AI notes cover only 10 meetings for the lifetime of the account, and that allowance does not renew. Amlin's free plan includes 4 meeting-hours that renew every month, a meeting in progress always finishes, your full note history is kept, and dictation is included and never metered.
