Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Amlin is a meeting notepad built on a simple promise: your notes are yours, and we keep as little as possible. This policy explains what we collect, why, and who processes it. Amlin is operated by Amlin Labs LLC ("we", "us").

The short version

  • We do not record or store your meeting audio. Audio is streamed to our transcription provider, converted to text in real time, and discarded. We never write audio files to disk or keep them.
  • Your notes and transcripts are yours. They are stored on your device and synced to your account so you can reach them from your other devices.
  • No bot joins your meetings. Amlin captures audio at the operating-system level; it does not join calls as a participant.
  • We use a small number of trusted providers to make the product work, listed below.

Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and a Google account identifier. We use this to authenticate you and to associate your notes with your account.

Your content

Notes you write and transcripts generated during meetings you choose to capture. This content is stored locally on your device and synced to our servers so it is available across your devices. We process it only to provide the service to you.

Meeting audio

When you start capturing a meeting, Amlin streams microphone and system audio to our transcription provider to produce a live transcript. The audio is not stored by Amlin — it is discarded once transcribed. Only the resulting text is kept, as part of your notes.

Calendar (optional)

When you sign in with Google, Amlin also requests read-only access to your Google Calendar to show your upcoming meetings and help you attach a note to an event. Calendar access is optional — you can decline it on Google's consent screen and still use Amlin. We do not modify your calendar, and we do not retain calendar data beyond what is needed to display it to you.

Usage information

We record aggregate usage — such as meeting-hours and the number of AI queries — to enforce plan limits and support billing. We keep basic diagnostic logs to operate the service reliably.

Payment information

Payments are handled by our payment processor, Stripe, which acts as Merchant of Record. We do not receive or store your full card details.

Website analytics & cookies

Our website (amlin.ai) uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors find and use the site — for example, which pages are visited, how visitors arrive, and the general region they visit from. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your device and browser type, approximate location, and the pages you view. We use this information only in aggregate to improve the site; we do not use it to identify you. You can learn how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser. If you visit from the EU/EEA or UK, we ask for your consent before loading Google Analytics, and no analytics cookies are set unless you accept; to change your choice later, clear this site's data in your browser and revisit. The Amlin app itself does not use Google Analytics.

Support chat

Our website uses Intercom to provide the chat messenger you can use to reach us. Intercom sets cookies that are needed to operate the messenger — for example, to keep your conversation as you move between pages — and processes the messages and any contact details you choose to share with us, along with basic device and browser information. You can read how Intercom handles this data in Intercom's Privacy Policy.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve Amlin.
  • To transcribe meetings and generate the notes you ask for.
  • To sync your content across your devices.
  • To enforce plan limits, process subscriptions, and prevent abuse.
  • To communicate with you about your account or service issues.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your notes or transcripts to train our own models.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Amlin accesses a limited set of Google user data — your basic profile (email address, name, and Google account identifier) to create and identify your account, and read-only access to your Google Calendar events so we can show your upcoming meetings and let you attach notes to them. Calendar access is optional: you can decline it on Google's consent screen and still sign in, and it is always read-only — Amlin never creates, edits, or deletes calendar events, and accesses this data only to provide these features to you.

Amlin's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke Amlin's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Data retention & deletion

Your notes and transcripts are kept until you delete them or close your account. Meeting audio is never retained. You can delete individual notes at any time in the app. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at support@amlin.ai and we will remove it, subject to any records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@amlin.ai. Because much of your content is stored locally on your device, you can also access and delete a great deal of it directly in the app.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Account credentials are stored using your operating system's secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

Children

Amlin is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
