Who owes what, by when

Every "Sarah will circulate the deck by Wednesday" is captured with the owner and the date, across every meeting, in one list. Chasing follow-ups stops being archaeology: the list is already sorted into overdue, today, and this week.

Questions assistants ask us

Will a bot show up in the exec's meetings?

No. Amlin captures audio from your Mac at the operating-system level, so nothing joins the call and nothing appears in the participant list. Board calls look exactly as they did before. Consent stays a human matter: recording laws vary by place, and our terms require that participants know the meeting is being captured. A standing line in the invite usually covers it.

Do I need to be in the meeting?

Yes. Amlin captures meetings that happen on your Mac, with you in them. It doesn't dial into meetings on its own or record ones you're not attending, and we think that's the right line for a tool like this.

Is this confidential enough for executive-level material?

Audio is never stored: it's transcribed live and discarded, and only the text remains, synced to your own account. No one else at the company can see your notes unless you share them yourself. The details are in our privacy policy, written to be read.

Does it matter which platform the meeting uses?

No. Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex, a phone call played through your Mac: if it makes sound on the machine, Amlin can capture it. There are no per-platform plugins to maintain.
