Amlin vs Fathom

Fathom is a meeting recorder: its notetaker joins your video call, records it, and stores the footage so you can rewatch and share clips. Amlin is a meeting notepad: it listens from your own Mac, writes everything up, and keeps no recording at all. Which one you want depends on whether you ever want the tape.

Facts checked August 2026 against Fathom's public pricing and documentation. Spot something outdated? Tell us.

The short answer

Fathom records unlimited Zoom, Meet, and Teams calls on its free plan but caps AI summaries at 5 per month. Premium is $20 a month (or $16 billed annually) per user, with Team and Business tiers for sales orgs that want CRM sync.

Amlin is $10 a month, works with anything that makes sound on a Mac, stores no audio or video, and folds dictation and a to-do list into the same app.

If you need the video, pick Fathom. If you need the notes, the privacy, and the follow-through, pick Amlin.

Which one fits you

Choose Amlin if…

  • You never want a client to see "Fathom Notetaker has joined" or wonder where the recording lives.
  • In your line of work, a stored video of every call reads as a liability.
  • Your meetings happen everywhere: Webex one day, a phone call through your Mac the next.
  • You want dictation and a cross-meeting to-do list in the same $10 app.

Choose Fathom if…

  • You want to rewatch calls and share video clips; that's Fathom's core strength.
  • You run a sales team that needs coaching metrics and CRM field sync.
  • You need Windows or mobile capture; Amlin is Mac-only.

Questions, answered

Is Amlin a good Fathom alternative?

If you work on a Mac and don't want a notetaker bot appearing in your calls, yes. Amlin captures audio from your own machine, works with any app that makes sound, never stores audio or video, and includes dictation and a cross-meeting to-do list for $10 a month. Fathom is the better fit if you want video recordings, clip sharing, or sales-team features, and we'd rather point that out than let you find out after a download.

Does Amlin record video like Fathom?

No, deliberately. Fathom stores the footage so you can rewatch and share clips, which is genuinely useful for sales coaching. Amlin makes the opposite trade: audio is transcribed live and discarded on the spot, nothing is ever recorded, and only your notes and transcript text remain. For client work under NDA, "there is no tape" is often the feature that matters. Details in our privacy policy.

Does Fathom work outside Zoom, Meet, and Teams?

Fathom's notetaker joins meetings on those three platforms. Amlin captures system audio at the operating-system level, so it works with them plus anything else that plays sound on your Mac: Webex, a client's webinar tool, a phone call on speaker.

How do the free plans compare?

Fathom's free plan records unlimited meetings but includes 5 AI summaries per month; past that you have recordings without write-ups. Amlin's free plan includes 4 meeting-hours per month (about 5 or 6 meetings) with the full write-up on every one, plus unmetered dictation and your complete note history.
