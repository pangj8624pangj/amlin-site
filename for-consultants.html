Confidentiality you can put in writing

Client NDAs get easier to honor when there's less to protect: Amlin never stores audio. Sound is transcribed live and discarded on the spot, and your notes sync only to your own account. When a client asks how their conversation is handled, our privacy policy is the answer, in plain language.

Every commitment, billed or kept

"I'll get you the revised model by Friday" is revenue when you deliver it and reputation when you don't. Amlin pulls each commitment out of the call with an owner and a due date, and keeps them in one list across every client, split into yours and what you're waiting on from them.

The whole engagement, on recall

Ask across your meeting history: "what did we agree on scope in the kickoff?", "when did they first mention the budget freeze?" Amlin answers with citations back to the exact line, so you walk into month three quoting month one.

The recap email, before you stand up

The write-up lands seconds after the call ends: summary, decisions, action items. Hold the dictation key, talk through your framing, and the follow-up email is drafted before your next meeting starts. Clients notice who sends the recap first.

Billable hours are for thinking

An hour spent reconstructing a call from memory is an hour the client shouldn't be paying for. Amlin gives it back: the notes write themselves up, the to-dos file themselves, and you spend the hour on the work you were actually hired for.

Questions consultants ask us

Do my clients need to install or approve anything?

No. Amlin runs on your Mac and captures system audio at the operating-system level, so there's nothing for the client to install and no bot requesting to join. One thing stays yours: recording-consent laws vary by place, and our terms require you to tell participants and follow the rules that apply to your calls. Most consultants handle it with one line at the top of the call.

Where do the notes and transcripts live?

On your device, synced to your own account so your other Macs can reach them. Audio is never stored, by us or on disk. Deleting a note deletes it from our servers too.

Does it work with whatever platform the client picks?

Yes. Zoom, Meet, Teams, and anything else that makes sound on your Mac, including a client's exotic webinar tool. There's no per-platform integration to break.

Can I keep engagements separated?

Every meeting is its own note, tied to the calendar event when you connect Google Calendar (read-only, optional). Ask can be scoped to a single meeting when you want answers from one engagement only.
