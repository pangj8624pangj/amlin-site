Why this matters more than it sounds

Most people speak around 150 words a minute and type around 40. Dictation should be the fastest way to get words out of your head, and with the built-in tool it often isn't, because you pay the speed back in editing. Once the cleanup is automatic, speaking becomes the default way to answer email, capture a task the moment you think of it, or draft the first version of anything. That's the habit Amlin's dictation is built for, and it's the same engine that writes up your meetings.

Questions, answered

Is Amlin's dictation private?

Yes. It runs entirely on your Mac: the audio never leaves the device, and the cleanup happens on-device too. This is a different path from Amlin's meeting transcription, which streams audio for live transcription and never stores it. The full picture is in our privacy policy.

Does dictation cost extra in Amlin?

No. System-wide dictation is included in the free plan and never metered. The free plan's 4 meeting-hours apply to meeting transcription only.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app with a text field: Mail, Slack, your code editor, your browser, a form on a website. Hold the dictation key, speak, and the finished text is typed where your cursor is.

When is the built-in dictation enough?

For a short sentence you'd have typed anyway, it's free and fine, and we'd say use it. The gap opens up in longer, messier speech: drafting emails, capturing tasks, thinking out loud. If you've tried dictating a full email with the built-in tool and gone back to typing, that's the experience Amlin exists to fix.
